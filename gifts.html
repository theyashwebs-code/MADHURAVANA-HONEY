Questions

Before you send

Can it be delivered straight to the person I’m gifting?

Yes. At checkout, enter the recipient’s address as the delivery address. Their name and your message are sent to us with the order.

How do I pay?

Cash on Delivery. You pay when the gift arrives, nothing upfront.

Can I order for many people at once?

Yes, up to 100 jars of each size here. For larger or multi-address orders, message us on WhatsApp and we’ll arrange it.

Is it safe for everyone?

Honey is wonderful for anyone over one year old. It should never be given to infants under 12 months, so it isn’t the right gift for a newborn.

How long will it take to arrive?

Orders within Kerala and Tamil Nadu usually arrive in 2 to 3 business days, and the rest of India in 4 to 7 business days. Order a little early for a special date.
