QUESTIONS

Asked, and answered.

01🍯PURITY

Is Madhurava honey 100% pure?

Absolutely. Madhurava honey is 100% pure, natural, and raw. We source it directly from trusted apiaries in the forests of Kerala and Tamil Nadu. It contains zero added sugar, corn syrup, artificial flavors, or preservatives.

02🧊CRYSTALLISATION

Why does my honey look cloudy or turn solid (crystallise)?

Crystallisation is the ultimate proof of 100% raw and pure honey. Real honey contains natural sugars and pollen that naturally solidify over time, especially in cooler weather. If your honey crystallises, simply place the jar in warm water for a few minutes to return it to its liquid state.

03🌿PROCESS

Is your honey pasteurised or heated?

No. We do not micro-filter or high-heat our honey. We handle it gently to ensure that all the natural enzymes, vitamins, and immunity-boosting benefits remain fully intact for your health.

04🌴ORIGIN

Where exactly does Madhurava honey come from?

We source our honey from the rich, bio-diverse forest regions and pristine landscapes of Kerala and Tamil Nadu. By partnering directly with traditional local bee-keepers, we ensure authentic taste while supporting ethical bee-keeping communities.

05💚HEALTH

What are the health benefits of Madhurava honey?

Because our honey is raw and unaltered, it is rich in natural antioxidants, active enzymes, and antibacterial properties. It serves as a natural energy booster, aids digestion, soothes sore throats, and acts as a wholesome replacement for refined sugar.

06📦SHIPPING

Why is there a shipping charge on single jars?

To support our local retail shop partners who stock Madhurava on their shelves, we maintain fair pricing across all channels. We charge a small shipping fee on individual website orders to offset delivery costs, but we love offering free shipping on our special multi-pack bundles!

07🥄STORAGE

How should I store Madhurava honey?

Store your jar at room temperature in a dry place, away from direct sunlight. Make sure the cap is tightly closed after every use. Do not refrigerate your honey, as cold temperatures speed up the natural crystallisation process.

08👶SAFETY

Can I give Madhurava honey to my infant or baby?

No. Pediatricians advise that infants under 12 months should never consume raw honey. This is a standard safety rule for all honey brands worldwide, as infant digestive systems are not yet mature enough to process the natural spores found in raw honey. For anyone over the age of one, it is completely safe, healthy, and highly beneficial.

09🚚DELIVERY

How long will it take for my order to arrive?

We ship directly from our hubs in South India. Orders within Kerala and Tamil Nadu typically reach your doorstep within 2 to 3 business days. For the rest of India, delivery generally takes 4 to 7 business days, depending on your location. We will email you a tracking link as soon as your jar of health is dispatched.

10↩️RETURNS

What is your return policy?

Because honey is a consumable food product, we cannot accept returns once the bottle has been shipped. However, your satisfaction and health are our priorities. If your order arrives damaged, leaking, or broken, please reach out to us within 48 hours of delivery, and we will immediately send you a replacement or issue a refund.

11✋ORDERS

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order as long as it has not been dispatched from our warehouse yet. Once the package is handed over to our courier partner and a tracking number is generated, the order cannot be cancelled.
